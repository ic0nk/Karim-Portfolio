Learning Outcomes LO1: Interactive Media Products

LO1: Interactive Media Products – Learning and Reflection

This learning outcome is focused on the design and creation of interactive media products. I explored how to design digital content that users can interact with, including websites and applications. Key areas I focused on include user experience (UX), interface design, and the use of digital tools to bring ideas to life. To reflect on my development, I’ve included 7 reviews, each highlighting what I learned, how I applied feedback, and how I improved. These reviews are clearly organized across three pages to make it easy to follow my journey.

When you visit this page, you'll first see a real example of an interactive project in action, followed by this introduction. As you scroll through, you’ll be able to explore each review and see how I applied design principles in real-world scenarios.

Review 1

GOAL:

My main goal was to design my personal portfolio in Figma in a way that reflects who I am as a designer. I aimed for a layout that is clean, professional, and easy to navigate, with a strong focus on visual hierarchy and user-friendly interaction. I wanted each section to clearly represent my personality, creativity, and the skills I’ve developed throughout my journey. In addition to the portfolio itself, I also worked on developing a visual brand for the site, including a consistent color scheme, logo, and typography that aligns with my design identity. I also began designing a mobile app as part of the project to explore more interactive design skills and to ensure my designs adapt across different platforms. View Result 

ACTION:

I began by researching different portfolio and branding examples to understand what makes a strong and appealing design. This included studying how others presented their work, structured their pages, and used visual elements to tell their story. From there, I created wireframes to plan out the basic structure of my portfolio, focusing on clarity and flow. I structured the layout in Figma using grids and alignment tools to maintain a professional and balanced appearance.

REFLECTION:

Throughout the project, I applied everything I was learning to my portfolio, making sure it reflected my personal design style while staying consistent with UX best practices. I also created a full brand guide for Owen Bryce, which included rules for logo usage, colour codes, font choices, tone of voice, and overall visual language. This brand guide made sure the identity could stay consistent in the future, across different media and platforms.
